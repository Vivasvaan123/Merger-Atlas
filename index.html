BEHIND THE ATLAS

Data & methods

This atlas connects measured galaxy morphology with fitted coordinates, catalog properties, images, and saved segmentation. It is an exploratory research interface, not a physical merger-stage diagnosis.

Representation

Loading provenance…

PCA uses processed morphology measurements, not direct image pixels. Axes, colors and filters do not refit the representation.

Measurements & colors

Catalog values are shown as stored. Mass/SFR units and logarithmic conventions have not been independently verified. The central-98% color scale uses the 1st and 99th percentiles of the fixed fitted cohort. Values outside those limits saturate at endpoint colors; missing colors are gray.

Legacy Fourier descriptors are retained from the source table; do not assume they are validated measures of boundary complexity. Saved votes follow the exported transform; calibration and training-target conventions require verification.

Images & masks

Display WebP previews use the exported stretch and are not photometric data. Masks are lossless class-label PNGs with matched dimensions. Failed masks are not guessed or truncated. Matched dimensions are not proof of physical registration.

Selection & sharing

Selection uses stable row identities and is retained across filters. A new lasso or box selection replaces the previous selection. Share links preserve the view and inspected object, not the selected sample. CSV includes stored exported values and fit identity.

Release status

Research preview. Scientific validation, source-unit checks, dataset citations, and redistribution documentation remain release responsibilities.
